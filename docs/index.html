Family Planner

A self-hosted organizer for the whole family: a shared calendar, chores, school lunches, work shifts and a kitchen wall display. It runs on your own homelab.

Install GitHub Releases
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mcross87/family-planner/main/proxmox/install.sh)"

Paste it into your Proxmox node's Shell. It creates the LXC, installs Docker and starts the planner.

Everything a busy family juggles

📺 Wall display

An old monitor and a Raspberry Pi show today, the week ahead, lunches and to-dos. It dims at night.

📱 On every phone

Add it to the home screen. Sign in with a PIN, Face ID or a fingerprint.

🧒 Kids' pages

Chores with weekly stars, their own to-dos, birthday countdowns and school-morning notifications.

🥪 School lunches

Plan school lunch or packed, day by day. Kids' favourite dishes are picked automatically.

💼 Work shifts

Tap days on a month grid, or type a week the way a paper schedule looks.

📅 Google Calendar

Shows your family's Google calendars and can copy the planner's appointments back.

🌙 Bedtime & homework

Checklists for school nights and weekends, a reminder for each step, and rewards the kids save their stars for.

📧 Teacher emails

Upload an email, PDF or photo. The planner finds the no-school days, events, gym days, forms and payments for you to tick.

🍽️ Meals

Plan suppers, share the grocery list, cook with what's in the house, and see this week's flyer deals near you.

💊 Medicine

Pill reminders, dose spacing for kids, puffer counters, temperatures and a printable report for the doctor.

🔔 Evening check

At 8 pm the adults get what tomorrow needs: school day, gym, lunches, appointments and anything not done.

💲 Bills

Due dates and reminders for adults only. They never show on the kids' pages.

🖥️ Homelab tab

Proxmox, AdGuard Home, hourly speed tests and the devices on your network, all in one place.

🔒 Private

One SQLite file on your server. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

Install in a few minutes

  1. Proxmox: run the line above in the node's Shell. Any Debian or Ubuntu machine: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mcross87/family-planner/main/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash
  2. Open http://<server>:8080/setup on your home network. Add your family, pick your town and set a family PIN.
  3. Optional: add a Cloudflare Tunnel or Tailscale for phones away from home, and a Raspberry Pi for the wall.

No internet on the Proxmox host's shell? Download a release file and run bash install.sh --release family-planner-v1.0.0.tar.gz. The README has every option.
