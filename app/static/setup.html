Welcome to your Family Planner

A few questions and you're ready. Everything can be changed later in Settings.

Open this page from your home network.

For safety, a new planner can only be set up from a device on the same network as the server, at http://<server address>:8080/setup.

1 · Your family

Kids get their own fun page, chores and the lunch planner. Colours show whose event is whose.

2 · Where you live

The town sets the weather on the wall screen. Time zone: (set when the planner was installed).

3 · Family PIN

Phones use it to sign in from outside your home (then Face ID or fingerprint). Pick one that isn't easy to guess, like 1234.

4 · From outside your home (optional)

If you've set up a Cloudflare Tunnel, Tailscale or similar. Leave blank for now if not; see the README.

Your family's information stays on your own server.
